Changelog 1.005.01: Big Chungus
Our Hero might be bodyless but that does not mean its physics can't be revamped, new and improved collisions to avoid exploits where bullets did not hit at certain angles and distance, corrected crouching speed bug, now sprinting is lost once the player stops moving. 
Revamped the weapon system, so our hero now spawns with just a pistol and each weapon can be unlocked or locked, has ammo and had damage tweaked.
Added Pick up system, with prefabs for regaining ammo, recovering health, and unlocking weapons and more.
Respawning players after death or at new levels lose all unlocked weapons. This can be fixed in another update,but bear in mind losing everything on death and very limited ammo can prove to be very fun, with situations where the player has to keep moving to get new ammo or health, makes death meaningful and progress tough.
